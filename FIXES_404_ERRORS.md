## How to Clear Browser Cache:

1. **Hard Refresh (Clear Cache):**
   - Windows/Linux: `Ctrl + Shift + Delete`
   - Mac: `Cmd + Shift + Delete`
   - Or: `Ctrl + F5` (most browsers)

2. **Or Open DevTools:**
   - Press `F12`
   - Go to Network tab
   - Check "Disable cache"
   - Refresh the page
